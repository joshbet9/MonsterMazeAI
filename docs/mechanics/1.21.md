# Monster Maze AI — 1.21 Mechanics Specification

## Source
MonsterMaze/1.21/MonsterMazeStandalone/src/main/java/me/monstermaze.

## Maze
The same 99×99 layouts and world conversion are used as 1.8. Raw paths are 1, 2, 5, 6; spawn 2; center 3, 4, 5, 6; barriers 4, 6. Runtime waypoint state is dynamic.

## Timing
Original: 60 seconds decreasing 2 seconds per stage to 15. Modern/Classic: max(15, 35 - ((stage - 1) * 20 / 9)). First arrival can shorten remaining time to min(current, max(6, 16 - (stage - 1))). All alive players on the active pad can reduce it to 4 seconds. Preview appears at 2 seconds. Center deterioration begins after 20 seconds and runs 11 steps.

Starter monsters: 150 Original; 225 Modern/Classic. Transition additions: +15 Original; +30 Modern/Classic.

## Monsters
Each monster has previous location, target waypoint and cardinal direction. When within 0.4 blocks of the target, cardinal neighbours are collected; an immediate reverse is removed when alternatives exist; otherwise a U-turn is allowed; one legal neighbour is chosen randomly. Directions are independent and player position does not select the route.

The implementation re-decides navigation at 10 Hz while actual movement remains tick-driven. The helper parameter is 0.8 in 1.21, but parity with the 1.8 implementation means the AI should model effective monster speed as equivalent to 1.8 rather than treating 0.8 as blocks/tick.

## Collision
Normal contact is approximately horizontal and 3D squared-distance < 1.0, deals 4 HP, has a 1 second player cooldown and launches using normalized monster→player horizontal direction with strength 1.0, vertical add 0.75, cap 1.2 and grounded bonus +0.2. Only one monster causes knockback at a time; Safe Pad players are immune.

Any kit can deliberately use monster contact as movement by controlling approach direction. Maverick redirects the launch toward the active Safe Pad, falling back to the preview pad where applicable.

## Jumping and movement
Jumper has 5 charges in Original and 3 in Modern/Classic. Vanilla jump strength is 0.42. Holding Space produces repeated grounded jumps while charges remain. Charge use is gated at 750 ms and recent monster-hit recovery prevents a knocked Jumper from losing a charge. Enhanced modes refill 3 on Safe Pad and make pad jumps free.

When jumping is locked, 1.21 uses jump-strength locking rather than the legacy negative Jump potion. Modern additionally gives locked players Speed II. Classic omits that speed boost. Therefore 1.8 and 1.21 require separate player movement simulators.

## Kits
- Slowballer: 16 maximum, +1 every 2 seconds; slows other players for 2 seconds and does not slow monsters. Cryo Blitz in enhanced modes: 6-block radius, 3-second freeze, 30-second cooldown; thaw returns normal route behaviour.
- Body Builder: first-to-pad +1 heart max health, cap 15 hearts; first arrival heals 4 HP, later arrivals 2 HP. Body Rush: 2 activations, 10 seconds each; contact deflects the monster, prevents normal player damage/knockback and consumes 2 seconds per real contact.
- Repulsor: 3 charges, 6-block radius; launches nearby living non-player entities away and removes them from normal navigation until cleanup.
- Maverick: enhanced modes only, directed padward knockback.

## AI consequence
The 1.21 adapter should expose exact tick state to the common planner while keeping player physics separate from the common monster/maze model. The planner must account for deliberate collisions, abilities, dynamic cells and stochastic monster occupancy.
