# Monster Maze AI — 1.8 Mechanics Specification

## Source
MonsterMaze/1.8/MonsterMazeStandalone, including the extracted source.

## Maze
- 99×99.
- worldX = centerBlockX - 49 + layoutRow.
- worldZ = centerBlockZ - 49 + layoutColumn.
- Path entity position is block centre at centerY.
- Raw paths: 1, 2, 5, 6. Spawn: 2. Center: 3, 4, 5, 6. Barriers: 4, 6.
- Runtime waypoint state can disable raw path cells.

## Timing
Original/Speed: 60 seconds initially, decreasing 2 seconds per stage to 15. Modern uses the faster 35-second progression toward 15. First arrival can shorten the remaining phase to max(6, 16 - (stage - 1)), subject to the current timer. All alive players on the pad can reduce it to 4 seconds. Preview appears around 2 seconds. Center deterioration begins 20 seconds after live start and uses 11 decay steps.

Monster additions: +15 per transition in Original/Speed and +30 in Modern. Starter population: 150 Original/Speed, 225 Modern.

## Monsters
Waypoint decisions are throttled to 10 Hz in the current 1.8 implementation while movement remains tick-driven. The movement helper receives 1.4 multiplied by the mode speed multiplier. At intersections the next cardinal path cell is independently randomized, avoiding an immediate U-turn when another option exists; U-turn is allowed when stuck.

## Collision
Normal contact deals 4 HP, has a 1 second player cooldown and launches the player using the normalized monster→player horizontal direction, strength 1.0, vertical add 0.75, cap 1.2 and grounded bonus +0.2. Only one monster produces knockback at a time. Safe Pad players are skipped.

A player can deliberately choose the approach direction, making monster knockback a route primitive.

## Jumper and legacy speeding
Jumper has 5 charges in Original and 3 in Speed/Modern. Holding Space repeats grounded jumps while charges remain. Charge use is gated at 750 ms and is protected during post-hit recovery. Enhanced modes restore 3 charges on Safe Pad and make pad jumps free.

Non-Jumpers/exhausted Jumpers use the legacy negative Jump effect (amplifier -10). The 1.8 implementation retains the original space-spam/speeding behaviour. Your observed behaviour: stationary spam gives no useful boost; running + Space produces momentum; rapid spam can reach roughly 1.5× normal movement depending on timing; correctly timed spam can cross a one-block gap but not wider gaps. This is an emergent 1.8 movement interaction, so the AI must model it tick-by-tick rather than as a fixed multiplier.

## Kits
- Slowballer: 16 maximum, +1 every 2 seconds; slows other players for 2 seconds; does not slow monsters. Enhanced Cryo Blitz: 6-block radius, 3-second freeze, 30-second cooldown; thaw returns monsters to normal movement.
- Body Builder: first-to-pad +2 HP max, cap 30 HP; first arrival heals 4 HP, later arrivals 2 HP. Enhanced Body Rush: 2 activations, 10 seconds each; qualifying contacts prevent normal damage/knockback and launch the monster away, consuming 2 seconds per real contact.
- Repulsor: 3 charges, 6-block radius; launches nearby living non-player entities away; launched monsters stop navigation and cannot hit during launch.
- Maverick: enhanced modes only; monster hits launch the player toward the relevant Safe Pad.

## AI consequence
The 1.8 simulator must reproduce legacy player physics, jump/spam timing, monster random walks, collision launches, dynamic cells, pad timing and kit resources before route search can be trusted.
